# 2026.230.9997 — ResolveDeliverableRef: one resolver for both delivery verbs

<!-- PLACEHOLDER CalVer: the pr-validator regenerates this stamp at merge. -->

Both image-delivery verbs need the same resolution and had each grown their own copy:
`charly vm cp-box` (VM venue) and `charly box load` (container venue). The bodies were
structurally identical and their justification comments near-verbatim rewords — found by
`charly#315`'s validator, in a cutover whose own thesis is that a second venue costs a
constructor, not a copy (R3).

`ResolveDeliverableRef(engine, input)` is that one resolver: an explicit ref that exists
locally is used exactly as authored — naming a tag IS the choice — and anything else goes
through the STRICT `ResolveBuiltImageRef`, which refuses to elect an image older than the
newest local build.

The strictness is easy to under-rate. Delivering a stale artifact is the wrong-artifact
class `charly check box` refuses to certify, and it is HARDER to notice at a delivery verb
than at a build one: the load succeeds and the venue simply runs the wrong image.

Coverage asserts both directions — an authored ref comes back untouched, and an
unresolvable one fails naming both the input and the remedy, so the operator is not sent
looking at the venue for a fault in their own build.
