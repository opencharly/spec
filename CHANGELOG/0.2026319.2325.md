# 0.2026319.2325 — feat(ir)!: the remaining four candy IR fields go opaque together

## Summary

The **remaining four** authored-shape fields on `#CandyModel` become opaque together (successor to #334, which auto-closed at the 5-verdict threshold with its findings fixed and a PASS on its final head) — `artifact`, `service`, `volumes`, `aliases`:

```cue
	service?: bytes  @go(Service,type=RawBody)
	volumes?: bytes  @go(Volumes,type=RawBody)
	aliases?: bytes  @go(Aliases,type=RawBody)
	artifact?: bytes @go(Artifact,type=RawBody)
```

**Why one PR and not four.** They are the *same mechanical conversion* that `hook` (`spec#331`) and `extract` (`spec#332`) already proved. Four separate PRs would spend four review cycles proving one thing four times — `DESIGN.md` R2 ("one implementation per behavior") and the BATCHING axis both point at one change, and one R10-style gate can honestly prove all four together because none of them changes behaviour in `spec` itself.

**The authored defs all STAY.** Each is `#Candy`'s own field (`candy.cue`), and the sweep confirms none of them loses its referent: what changes is that the IR stops **importing** them (I1 / D-SCH-7).

**The reader sets, swept read-only across all 392 materialized gitlinks.** Exactly 12 repos mention `CandyModel`, and the accounting reconciles to 12:

**`volumes`/`aliases` cost no adapter decode.** `spec_candy_adapter.go:184-187` reads the **`CandyView`** twins, not the model (the file's own comment says so at `:180-183`), so their opacity costs one *encode* in the producer plus the `plugin-box` model-site decodes. Their `HasContent` terms read `v.*` and are unaffected.

**The two BLOCKED-BY rows are about the DEF MOVE, not this change.** `#CandyView`/`#ResolvedBoxView` keep their authored `[]CandyVolume`/`[]CandyAlias` — that is correct and untouched here; it only means those two defs cannot leave `spec` until those views are themselves converted. That is the next layer of the same work, recorded in `PROGRAM/schema-ownership-rca-rdd.md` §7c.

## Change classification

Kind: a schema (IR) change in `spec`. It breaks consumers reading these four fields as authored slices — **two repos, per the sweep**: `sdk` (the adapter accessors and the producer) and `plugin-box` (five model sites for `service`, four for `volumes`/`aliases`).

**Breaking change + rollback:** consumers decode through the shared crossing (`marshalOpaque` in `loaderkit`, `decodeOpaque` in `deploykit`); `plugin-box` can either decode or read the adapter it already holds as `vc.dk[name]`. Rolling back restores the four slice types and regenerates; the wire is unchanged either way.
