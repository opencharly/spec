# 0.2026280.2214 — feat(spec)!: retire the dead #Workflow IR pair (R5, spec#192)

## Summary

The contract carried **two contract-level normalized-IR defs nothing produced or consumed**. A pipeline engine resolves an authored `kind: pipeline` into its **own** form — `plugin-pipeline` lowers it to `.opencharly/pipelines/<name>/{workflow.lobster,charly.yml}` and `plugin-lobster` executes that pair — so a contract-level "engine-agnostic IR" document was a shape with no producer and no consumer, described as if it were the live engine wire.

**Deleted (this is the R5 sweep and the guidance fix in one act — the doc claims WERE the defect):**

- `#Workflow` and `#WorkflowStep` from `schema/workflow.cue`.
- The header narrative that made them read as the live wire (*"the engine-agnostic IR every consumer lowers from"*, plus the lobster/GitHub-Actions consumer table).
- The three `#WorkflowStep` comparisons in `schema/pipeline.cue` (the "NORMALIZED IR this lowers to", the `#PipelineApproval` "the IR … always carries this object form", and the `#PipelineArms` "both `#PipelineStepBase` and `#WorkflowStep` embed …" paragraph) — each re-anchored to what is true: an **engine** normalizes; `#PipelineArms` is embedded by `#PipelineStepBase`, with `#PipelineSubStep` re-declaring its own non-recursive subset.

**Kept, unchanged — every def an engine actually uses:** `#WorkflowApproval` (`plugin-lobster/run.go:214` builds it), `#WorkflowStepResult`, `#WorkflowSchedule`, `#WorkflowTrigger`, the six request/reply envelopes, `#WorkflowScheduleRequest` / `#WorkflowScheduleEntry` / `#WorkflowScheduleReply`, `#WorkflowEngineCapability`.

**Scope correction worth reading: this is the dead PAIR, not the dead triple the ruling named.** `charly task cue-gen` refused the triple: `#WorkflowRunReply.steps: reference "#WorkflowStepResult" not found`. `#WorkflowStepResult` is live wire — carried by the reply of the engine that is running today — and a Go-only reference grep cannot see that CUE edge. It is kept, with a comment saying why it survives.
