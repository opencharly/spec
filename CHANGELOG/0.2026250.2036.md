## Summary

Parser-consolidation F1 wave (the R3 sweep from plan/nested-capture-architecture.md, Cutover F F1 block) — spec-side slice.

1. **Drop `ProjectLoader.ResourceChildren` from the seam interface** (F1.2). The host's deploy-shape composite (`foldSubstrateKind`'s `pl.IsDeployShape(pn) || len(pl.ResourceChildren(pn)) > 0` arm) is deleted; `IsDeployShape` now carries the member channel (any parsed member child classifies a node as deploy-shaped — the ONE `memberDisc`/`IsResourceDisc` predicate, which also covers structural/external members the frozen `#ResourceKind` walk missed). 2. **Fix the `#CheckEnv` `candy_dirs` CUE spelling** (`{string: string}` → `{[string]: string}`). RCA of a failing `TestGenReproducible`: commit e3c9d9e (#121) hand-edited `spec/cue_types_gen.go` to `map[string]string` ("generated mirror updated by hand in the generator's exact style") — `cue exp gengotypes` renders `{string: string}` as a broken `struct{String any}`. The `{[string]: string}` spelling renders as `map[string]string` (the wire intent, matching every sibling open-map field), so the regenerated mirror is byte-identical to the committed form and the drift is gone with the generator itself emitting the map.
