## Summary

`opencharly/spec#309`: the question *"which `add_candy:` refs does this deploy contribute, and at what composition scope?"* had three hand-written answers. This adds the ONE mechanism — `(*Deploy).ScopedAddCandyRefs(scope string) []ExtraCandyRef` in `spec/deploy_addcandy.go`, beside the member-tree accessors it walks (`member_tree.go`) and the scope constructors it tags with (`resolve_opts.go`).

The three consumers it must serve:

| consumer | site |
|---|---|
| the host's plugin-LOADING collection | `charly/charly/plugin_loader.go:1164` (`deployNodePluginContext`) |
| the pod-overlay build | `charly/charly/build_overlay.go:360` (`overlayCandies`) |
| the externalized `check` command's resolved-project widening | `opencharly/plugin-check`'s `addCandyRefsForEntity` (opencharly/plugin-check#147) |

The last one exists because a plugin cannot import core, and neither `ResolvedProject` nor `spec.CheckLoadPluginsReply` carries the refs (`go doc … ResolvedProject | grep -ci extracandy` → 0; the reply is empty by design), so `ResolvedProjectRequest.ExtraCandyRefs` — a REQUEST field — is the only channel and every caller must supply the input. Without a shared home each caller hand-writes the loop; that is the duplication this removes.

**The traversal contract** (the thing all three must agree on, and what the test pins):
- a member authored INSIDE the kind body (`Member.InSubstrate()`) is deployed into its parent's venue, is part of its parent's composition, and shares its scope;
- a DEPLOY-LEVEL member (`Member.Alongside()` — a sibling of the kind key) is its own composition, addressed by its own name, and is **not** visited;
- `scope` is the caller's, normally `spec.BoxScope(<deploy name>)`, because a deploy IS its box — a constant label is the `charly#739` false-conflict bug (two independent deploys legitimately pin one candy at different tags).

**What this does NOT do:** it does not migrate the two `charly` call sites. Those are that repo's own write scope in the campaign that found this (`charly-plugins` is live in `charly`), and `opencharly/spec#309` stays open until they consume this and delete their loops. This PR is the shared home the consumers need, which is the part that must exist first.
