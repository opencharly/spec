## Summary

Add `refs.GitClient.SetBypass(bool)` — the persisted form of the git-ref cache bypass. The flag lives in the `cache: git:` section of the per-host charly.yml (`bypass: true`), so a FRESH GitClient (a new process) honors it at construction. This is the mechanism behind the upcoming `charly cache bypass` operator surface (the plugin-cache command candy): the bypass becomes plugin-owned state, not a core CLI flag.

- `refs/git_client_test.go`: `TestGitClientSetBypassPersists` — a fresh client honors `SetBypass(true)` and resumes after `SetBypass(false)`.
