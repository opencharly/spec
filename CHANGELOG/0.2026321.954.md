# 0.2026321.954 — fix(vm)!: name the libvirt video-device and filesystem-driver vocabularies

## Summary

`schema/vm.cue` held two vocabularies under names DESIGN.cue spends on different concepts:

| design word | DESIGN.cue's concept | what vm.cue declared under it |
|---|---|---|
| `_device` / `_deviceWord` | the §9 **host device nodes** a need asks a venue for (`kvm`, `render`, `fuse`, `tun`, `vhost_net`, `vsock`, `hwrng`, `kfd`) | libvirt **video model** names (`virtio-vga`, `vhost-user-gpu`, …) |
| `_driver` / `_driverWord` | the §17.6 **driver table** (`podman`, `libvirt`, `qemu`, `kind`, …) | libvirt **filesystem driver** names (`virtiofs`, `9p`, `path`) |

CUE merges two definitions of one name SILENTLY. With a port that declares DESIGN's tables, the merged lists evaluated to `_deviceWord` = 14 words and `_driverWord` = 17, **exit 0**, and neither `cue-gen` nor `go build` refused it — the vocabulary was wrong and nothing said so.

Both take names that say what they feed, following this file's own `_libvirt*` convention (`_libvirtGraphicsType`, `_libvirtVideoDriver`, `_libvirtHostdevType`):

- `_device`/`_deviceWord` → `_libvirtVideoDevice`/`_libvirtVideoDeviceWord` (`#LibvirtVideo.device`)
- `_driver`/`_driverWord` → `_libvirtFilesystemDriver`/`_libvirtFilesystemDriverWord` (`#LibvirtFilesystem.driver`)

A comment at each table now names who owns `_device`/`_driver` (DESIGN's §9 host nodes and §17.6 driver table). The authored fields `device:` and `driver:` keep their names, so the wire is untouched.

One file, and it frees both names for the capability-ask port (opencharly/spec#355) — which is why it lands on its own, on `main` as it stands, rather than behind the unmerged `feat/cue-module` that deletes the two tables.
