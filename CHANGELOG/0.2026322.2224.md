## What this deliberately does not do, and why

Two rows the same census lists are **not** touched: `#StrVal` (`string | number | bool`) and
`#PackageItem` (scalar-or-record). I converted both, ran the suite, and the S3 convention test
refused them, because a vocabulary table's rows must be **words** and these rows are a type and a
constraint:

```console
$ go test ./schema/ -run TestEveryTableDerivesItsWordList

        cannot convert non-concrete value !="" (and 4 more errors)
```

So they are two of the four shape classes filed as **opencharly/spec#385** — a value union and a
heterogeneous container — and they wait for that DESIGN decision rather than being forced here.
Neither line is touched by this branch's diff, which is measured rather than asserted:

```console
$ git diff origin/main...HEAD -- schema/00_common.cue | grep -cE '^[-+].*(StrVal|PackageItem)'
0
```

(An earlier revision of this branch restored both definitions but transcribed their trailing
comment and closing indentation differently from `origin/main`; that is repaired in `446e8e58`, and
the count above is the measurement that the repair is complete.)
The other two classes in #385 (a default-bearing enum refused by `or()`, an absence-discriminated
arm refused by a computed `_|_`) are what keep `#Step` and `#VmSource` out of this PR entirely.

The remaining `union` and `absence` rows in these two files, and every row in `vm.cue`, fall into
one of those four classes; `#385` is where that is recorded.
